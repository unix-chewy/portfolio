Background

The short
version.

Hi, I'm Joney—a 4th-year CS student at Angeles University Foundation. I build ML systems that move beyond demos to solve real-world problems in agriculture and health. For my thesis, I took part on engineering and deploying a computer vision pipeline that helps farmers score rice leaf diseases in the field.

I am finishing a BS in Computer Science at Angeles University Foundation, where I have been named College Scholar for five semesters and University Scholar for one.

The thesis put me in the middle of a real problem: rice leaf disease is hard to score by eye, and farmers need it scored often. That is where the segmentation model came from, and where the 95.58 leaf IoU had to be defended in front of PhilRice and DOST rather than just reported—a rigor that ultimately earned the study's acceptance for presentation and publication at SOICT 2026. Before and alongside that, I spent three years keeping call-centre hardware alive, which is where I learned that a tool is only finished when someone who did not build it can use it.
